# DRAFT — PIKAS terms of service (for owner and legal review)

> **This is not a legal document and makes no legal claim.**
>
> - It is an outline of the topics the terms must cover, each tied to how the product behaves today (code as of
>   2026-10-25), so counsel can draft accurate terms.
> - Every **[DECIDE]** marker is for the owner or legal counsel.
> - Do not publish until legal approves.

## Parties and scope

- Palmchat Innovations LLC provides PIKAS to a customer (school group, school or Cafetería operator).
  **[DECIDE]** The contracting entity and the governing law.
- The customer's administrators create and manage their users. Each user acts within the role and scope they were
  given.

## Accounts and access

- **Credentials:** users keep them private. PIKAS never asks for a password, including in support.
- **Suspension:** PIKAS can suspend a customer's access (data preserved, reversible) or an individual sign-in.
  **[DECIDE]** The grounds and the notice period.

## The school's responsibilities

- Accuracy of student data, and authority to share it with a Cafetería. The school chooses which categories it shares.
- Authorization from guardians for dietary-restriction data and family access. **[DECIDE]** This needs a data
  processing agreement.

## The Cafetería's responsibilities

- Sales, prices, products and its cash. The POS records every sale with a receipt; closed register sessions cannot be
  reopened.
- **After financial activation:** cash collected for PIKAS Cash, held and settled weekly with PIKAS.
  **[DECIDE]** The agency terms (see the activation runbook's Gate 0).

## PIKAS Cash

- **What it is:** a prepaid balance per student, used at the Cafeterías the school shares with. Every movement is
  recorded and never erased; corrections are new movements.
- **Fee:** the replenishment fee is set by a versioned policy. Policy v1 is 300 basis points, rounded half-up; the
  wallet receives the net.
  **[DECIDE]** Whether the fee is borne by the family, the school or the Cafetería, how it is shown, and tax.
- **[DECIDE]** Refunds of unused balances, inactive balances, and what happens when a student leaves.
- **[DECIDE]** Custody and licensing (activation runbook, Gate 0).

## Settlement (financially activated customers only)

- **Week:** Monday to Sunday, America/Santo_Domingo, due the following Tuesday. PIKAS records settlements and payment
  confirmations; it does not move money itself.
- **[DECIDE]** Payment method, late payment, disputes («revisión»).
