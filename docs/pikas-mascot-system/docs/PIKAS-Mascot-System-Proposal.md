# PIKAS Mascot System Proposal

Oct 22, 2026 · @Edwin Jaquez

PIKAS should be a guide that explains states, not decoration: one canonical character, 35 planned assets, 12 motion patterns, and a first pack of 11 poses built on a vector redraw of today's logo. The product stays the hero; PIKAS points at it.

## Reference used

Analysed from the two logo files live on [pikas-pikas.app](https://pikas-pikas.app/): the logo mark (322 × 316 px PNG) and the horizontal lockup (882 × 334 px PNG). Colours below are read by eye from compressed rasters and are approximate until checked against source files.

**Biggest finding: the current mascot has no hat.** Owner decision: a hat is added as a new canonical element, worn only in role categories (section 2b).

**Second finding: only the head and one paw exist.** The mark is cropped at the shoulders, so there is no canonical body, legs or full-figure proportion yet. Designing that body is step 0 of the first pack.

**Owner decisions (Oct 22, 2026):**

1. **Hat** — new canonical element, on only for role categories: Cafetería, POS and Onboarding / guidance.
2. **Props** — the card is not mandatory. PIKAS also carries a yellow flower, because real pikas gather flowers for their winter haypile; in PIKAS the flowers are what it collects as value.
3. **Master vector** — none exists. The vector master is redrawn from the current PNG mark (step 0, section 12).
4. **Hex values** — chosen in this proposal (section 2c), contrast-checked.
5. **Identity** — PIKAS is and stays a pika. No mouse, hamster or generic-rodent drift.

## 1. Analysis of the current mascot

PIKAS is a golden pika with oversized round ears, a cream muzzle and a wide open smile, holding a teal contactless card, drawn in flat colour with a thick navy outline inside a teal rounded-square tile.

| Trait | What the art shows | Implication for the system |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Species | Pika (small round-eared lagomorph); many viewers will read it as a mouse or hamster | Lean into pika cues (rounded ears, compact body, no long tail) so it never becomes a generic mouse |
| Silhouette | Wide rounded head + two large round ears; whiskers break the outline on both sides | Ears + whiskers are the silhouette; they must survive at 24 px |
| Proportions | Head fills about 70% of the mark; body shown only to the shoulder | Full-body ratio must be designed (rule proposed in section 4) |
| Head shape | Broad, slightly squared jaw with soft cheek points; two small pointed tufts at the crown | Keep jaw, cheek points and tufts; they separate PIKAS from round stock mascots |
| Ears | Large, round, cream inside, navy rim, set high and wide | Primary recogniser; never shrink, point or droop them |
| Eyes | Small solid navy ovals, wide-set, one white highlight each | Fixed; expression comes from lids and head tilt, not eye shape |
| Nose / mouth | Small navy triangle nose on a cream muzzle; open smile with pink tongue | Open smile is the default; a closed smile is the quiet variant |
| Whiskers | Three navy strokes per side, extending past the head | Mandatory at all sizes above 32 px; reduce to two per side below |
| Paw | Rounded mitten paw, no visible fingers, gripping the card | Gestures must read with mitten paws (thumb only) |
| Prop | Teal card with yellow chip and white contactless waves | It is PIKAS Cash in the logo itself; make it the signature prop |
| Line | Heavy uniform navy outline, rounded joins, same weight inside and out | Line weight scales as a fixed share of figure height |
| Colour (approx.) | Yellow \~#FFC52E · cream \~#FFF3DF · navy \~#0F1F4B · teal \~#17C3C8 · tongue \~#F27C7C | Final values in section 2c; navy doubles as the monochrome colour |
| Shading | None: fully flat fills | All new poses stay flat; no gradients or highlights beyond the eye glints |
| Detail | About 15 distinct shapes | Low budget; props must be equally simple |
| Tone | Friendly, bright, clearly for students and families | Good base; poses and restraint add the "competent" half |
| Wordmark | Bold rounded geometric caps in navy; the A carries a yellow triangle | The yellow triangle is a reusable accent (badges, Demo mark) |

**Production notes on the current files:** both PNGs sit on a light grey box rather than transparency, show compression artifacts at the edges, and the line weight wobbles slightly. A clean vector redraw, matched exactly to the current art, is needed before any pose work. That also removes the soft "AI illustration" texture the brief wants to avoid.

## 2. Canonical character traits

The canonical PIKAS is the current mark, redrawn in vector without changes; every new asset is that character in a new pose. The identity core:

- **Ears** — large, round, cream-lined, high and wide.
- **Face** — small navy eyes with glints, triangle nose, cream muzzle, three whiskers per side.
- **Colour and line** — golden yellow body, heavy navy outline, flat fills.
- **Head shape** — broad jaw, cheek points, crown tufts.
- **The card** — the signature prop for real money; the yellow flower is its partner for value and collecting (section 2b).

The flexible layer is everything poses need: body posture, paw gestures, other props, head tilt, mouth open or closed.

## 2b. Hat and signature props

PIKAS has five canonical additions on top of the logo: a role hat and four money props — the card, the wallet, the coin purse and the yellow flower. Each has a fixed design and a fixed set of contexts.

**The hat — "gorra PIKAS"**

- **Design:** a soft navy cap with a short teal brim and the wordmark's yellow triangle on the front panel. Same navy outline and flat fill as the body.
- **Fit:** sits on the crown between the ears, tipped slightly back. Both ears and the crown tufts stay fully visible; the cap is never wider than the gap between the ears. Cap height ≈ 18% of head height.
- **On in:** C. Onboarding / guidance, D. Cafetería, E. POS, and website chapters 1–4, where PIKAS is working as the guide.
- **Off in:** A. Foundation (the canonical hatless reference), B. Success, F. Finance, G. Support (headset instead), H. Empty states, I. Demo (goggles instead), and the app icon / logo mark.
- **Small sizes:** at 32–48 px the cap stays but drops the triangle; below 32 px no cap.
- **Never:** two headpieces at once, other hat styles (chef, graduation, party), or the cap covering the ears.

**The card — money that is real**

The teal contactless card from the logo, with yellow chip and white waves. It stands for PIKAS Cash and actual payments: wallet, recargas, POS, receipts, transactions.

**The wallet — the account**

A navy wallet with a teal card peeking from the top and the yellow triangle on the front. It stands for the PIKAS Cash account itself: balance, wallet home, family top-ups and account history. Used with the closed smile, in the restrained finance tone.

**The coin purse — today's money**

A small teal purse with a flower emblem; a compact zip coin purse with a navy zipper and a yellow pull tab (owner pick, Oct 22). It stands for spending money for the day: the daily limit ("Para hoy"), a student's pocket view, small purchases and the allowance a family sets. Friendlier than the wallet; used in student-facing screens.

**Which prop when:** the wallet holds the account, the purse holds today's money, the card pays, the flowers are what PIKAS collects. One prop per pose.

**The flower — what PIKAS collects**

Real pikas gather flowers and grasses into a haypile for winter. PIKAS does the same: the yellow flower is what it collects, its friendly symbol of value, saving and progress.

- **Design:** five rounded petals in Flower Yellow with the navy outline, a Flower Orange centre, a short Leaf Green stem with one leaf. Drawn as simply as the card.
- **Use for:** student-facing moments, collecting and progress (setup steps gathered, savings goals), success, welcome, empty states (an empty haypile), and website storytelling.
- **The haypile:** a small pile of flowers is the visual for "everything gathered in one place"; it closes the website story (section 7).
- **Rules:** one signature prop per pose (card or flower, not both) in the app; the website may show the flower-to-card exchange once. The flower never represents an exact amount, never appears beside real balances or on finance confirmations, and copy never sets an exchange rate (no "1 flor = RD$…").

## 3. Personality

**PIKAS is the capable colleague who already knows where everything is.** Warm, quick, a little playful, and never in the way.

- **Core line:** "Te muestro." (I'll show you.) PIKAS points, presents and confirms — it does not perform.
- **Warm, not cute.** Friendly to a 9-year-old student and a 50-year-old cafeteria manager alike.
- **Competent.** Poses show knowing what to do: holding the right tool, pointing at the right place.
- **Playful in proportion.** Full expression on the website and onboarding; quieter in operations; restrained in finance.
- **Honest.** When something is wrong, PIKAS looks attentive and helpful, never comic.

**Tone dial by surface**

| Surface | Expression level | PIKAS feels like |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Public website, marketing | High | Host and storyteller |
| Onboarding, empty states | Medium | Guide |
| School / Cafetería admin | Medium–low | Helpful colleague |
| POS | Low | Quiet signal |
| PIKAS Cash, finance | Lowest | Precise assistant |
| Errors, security, money failures | None (no mascot) | — |

**Avoid:** big open-mouth laughs, sports-mascot fists, winking, sweat drops, dizzy eyes, crying, "oops" faces near money.

## 4. Consistency rules

One rule set for every artist and every AI-assisted variant; anything outside it is rejected, not adjusted.

| Area | Rule |
| --- | --- |
| Proportions | Full body = head 45% of total height (about 1 : 1.2 head to body). Ears add on top, not counted. Short, rounded body, no visible neck, short stubby legs, no long tail. Squash-and-stretch in motion ≤ 5%. |
| Expression range | Six faces: open smile (default), closed smile, warm (eyes softened into arcs), focused (mouth small, slight lean in), curious (head tilt 10–15°), attentive (eyes unchanged, ears up, mouth neutral). Two timeout-only faces: worried (raised brows, wobbly mouth) for a session about to end, and bored (half-lids, flat mouth) for a long idle in student views. No frowns, tears, sweat drops or shock faces. |
| Allowed poses | Standing, waving, pointing (L / R / up), presenting (open paw), holding a prop at chest height, holding a badge, both arms up, thinking (paw to chin), looking (head turn), sitting. |
| Paws | Mitten paws as in the logo: rounded, thumb only, no separate fingers. A point = mitten with the thumb side leading, never a raised single digit. No thumb or finger gestures at all: approval is shown with the check badge (SUC-01). Check every hand gesture at 48 px for any rude or ambiguous reading before approval. |
| Props | Flat, same outline weight, from the palette; one prop per pose. The card and the flower are the house props; others (tray, receipt, checklist, magnifier) follow its style. No real brands. |
| Perspective | Front and three-quarter only in product UI. Side view allowed on the website for walking sequences. |
| Line | Navy outline at a fixed 3% of figure height; inner lines the same weight. Never a hairline, never fixed px. |
| Shading | Flat only, matching the logo. Eye glints are the only highlight. |
| Colour | Yellow body, cream ears / muzzle / belly, navy line and eyes, teal for props and tile. Status colours (green, amber, red) only on props or badges, never on PIKAS. |
| Headwear | Hatless by default; the gorra PIKAS is worn only in the categories set in section 2b. Role accessories (apron, headset, lab goggles pushed up) are allowed only where a set below calls for them, and never cover the ears. |
| Props obscuring | A prop may cover up to 30% of the body; never the eyes, ears or whiskers. |
| Small sizes | ≤ 48 px: head-only mark (ears, face, whiskers at 2 per side, no card). Minimum 24 px; below that use the wordmark. |
| Large sizes | Detail cap = logo's shape count + one prop. No texture, fur strokes or 3D rendering. |
| Tile | The teal rounded square belongs to the app icon and logo mark. Poses ship as free-standing figures on transparency. |

**Never changes:** ear shape and size, eye shape and spacing, triangle nose and cream muzzle, whisker count (3 per side above 32 px), crown tufts, golden yellow body, navy outline weight, flat fills, head-to-body ratio, the cap's design whenever it is worn. If a pose only works by changing one of these, the pose is wrong, not the rule.

## 7. Website: El valle de PIKAS

The public site is one continuous scroll through a flower valley: PIKAS walks the valley collecting flowers, and at each stop a flower cluster blends into a real product screen. The flowers PIKAS gathers are the thread that connects School, Families, Cafetería, POS and PIKAS Cash, and the story ends with the whole valley, and the whole platform, connected.

**How it moves (the scroll grammar)**

- **Pinned chapters.** Each chapter pins to the screen while scrolling scrubs its animation, then releases into the next, in the style of Apple's classic product pages.
- **Blend, don't cut.** One image becomes the next: petals scatter and re-form as rows of a list, a purse opens into the daily-limit card, flowers drop into a wallet that zooms into the phone screen.
- **Layered depth.** Sky, far hills, mid hills, path and foreground flowers move at different speeds for parallax; PIKAS walks on the path layer.
- **Product stays the hero.** At each stop the screen grows to fill most of the frame and PIKAS steps to the edge (20% of the frame at most).
- **One world.** The loader (LOAD-01) is the same valley ground and haypile, so the first thing visitors see is the start of the story.

| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 0. Hero — *La cafetería escolar, por fin conectada.* | Morning over the valley; PIKAS waves from the path | Loader haypile settles into the hero field | Wallet phone and POS, side by side | FND-03 wave |

**Build notes:** CSS scroll-driven animations with a GSAP ScrollTrigger fallback; product screens as real HTML or high-resolution captures, never redrawn; PIKAS and the valley layers as SVG, with Lottie only for the walk cycle. Under reduced motion, each chapter is a static illustrated panel with its screen, and nothing scrubs.

**Guardrails:** every chapter still reads with PIKAS removed; animation pauses when scrolling stops; the demo disclaimer ("Experiencia de demostración. No procesa dinero real.") stays visible text, never mascot speech; the flowers never carry amounts or exchange rates.

## 8. Motion vocabulary

Fourteen patterns cover every animated asset; anything not on this list is not animated. Default easing is a soft ease-out (cubic-bezier 0.2, 0.8, 0.2, 1); nothing overshoots more than 4%.

## 11. Accessibility

PIKAS never carries information on its own: every state it appears in is fully stated in text beside it.

- **Decorative by default.** In-app mascot images use `alt=""` (or `aria-hidden="true"` on inline SVG) because the heading and body text already say what the state is.
- **Meaningful only when it adds content.** If an illustration shows something not in the text (a website chapter showing the card tapping a student row), give a short alt such as "PIKAS conecta la tarjeta con el perfil del estudiante." Describe the action, not the character's looks.
- **Never the only signal.** Errors, warnings, offline state and success always have text and, where relevant, an icon. The mascot's face never stands in for a status colour or word.
- **Reduced motion.** Honour `prefers-reduced-motion`: every pattern in section 8 has a static fallback; scroll-scrubbed website motion becomes a crossfade between static poses.
- **No looping beside input.** Loops stop within 10 s or 3 cycles and never run next to an active form field (WCAG 2.2.2).
- **Contrast.** Navy outline on white is well above 3:1; on dark navy backgrounds use the dark mode keyline (section 9). Yellow on white is low contrast, so the outline, not the fill, must define the shape.
- **Size floor.** No mascot below 24 px; below that, use the wordmark or nothing.
- **Language.** Alt text in the interface language (Spanish by default), written by people, not generated.
